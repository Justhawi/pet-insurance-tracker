      How to read this panel

      What each number means and where it comes from

      The two scores

      1. Ponderated score — each platform weighted by its number of reviews. This is the fair one: it does not let a handful of five-star reviews outweigh thousands.
      2. Average score — the plain mean of Trustpilot and Google. Easy to explain, but blind to volume.
      3. Opinions — total reviews behind the score. Below a few hundred, treat the score as an indication rather than a verdict.

      Freshness

      1. Google Maps — refreshed live from this site through the Places API when a key is configured.
      2. Trustpilot — a snapshot published from the office PC. Trustpilot blocks data-centre traffic, so it is never overwritten with a blocked read.
      3. Refresh — the button on Overview asks the server to re-check. On the free Render plan the service sleeps after inactivity; the first load after that takes a moment.
